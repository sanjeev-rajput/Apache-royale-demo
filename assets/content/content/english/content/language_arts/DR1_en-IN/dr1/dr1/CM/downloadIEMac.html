Destination Reading cannot find the required Macromedia Flash Player
Macromedia Flash Player version 6.47 (or higher) is required to run Destination Reading.

Please follow these steps to install the latest Macromedia Flash player;

1) Click here to go to Macromedia's Flash player installation page.

2) Install the Flash player as per instructions on that page. (You may be instructed to close your browser before installation.)

3) After a sucessful installation, Flash will open your browser at a Flash test page.

4) Close your browser and restart Destination Reading. Click here to close this window..
